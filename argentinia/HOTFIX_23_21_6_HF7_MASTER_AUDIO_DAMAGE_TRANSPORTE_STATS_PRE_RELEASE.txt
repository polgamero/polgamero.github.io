Base: HF6 cumulative PRE-RELEASE. Engine 23.21.6; mp-23.19.2; econ-23.19.5.6; schema 10; Functions 41; Rules 23.13.86; physical pool 900 / published baseline 880.

HF7 DELTAS
1. Quick speaker icon is now MASTER audio mute
   - Correction from field report: music mute itself was working on iPhone; SFX were intentionally independent. This is NOT treated as an iOS audio defect.
   - Product decision: the quick 🔊/🔇 controls in main menu and gameplay now mute/unmute BOTH music and SFX.
   - Master mute is persisted separately (`masterMuted`) and does NOT overwrite the independent Music/SFX enabled flags or volume sliders in Options.
   - Muting immediately pauses/silences managed music and any active SFX; future SFX are blocked while master mute is active. Unmute resumes only the music channel if its own preference remains enabled; old SFX are never replayed.

2. iOS/WebKit player-damage transient hardening
   - The friend report described a strange transient message near/below the player badge after creatures dealt damage, but no screenshot or literal matching text exists in the log/source.
   - Telemetry DOES prove normal player damage events (including Paramédica 1 + Cupé 5 in the same combat) and no engine-state corruption.
   - The plausible presentation risk is a WebKit transient animation node surviving longer than intended if `Animation.finished` is delayed/stalled.
   - Web Animations now have a deadline fallback; floating player-damage nodes also have an independent TTL removal, `aria-hidden`, nowrap, and containment. This is presentation-only and cannot alter damage totals or rules.

3. Transporte/Vehículo printed P/T is globally visible
   - All 10 physical Transportes/Vehículos already carry canonical printed `baseStats.power/toughness`.
   - Shared `createCardElement` now shows that printed P/T badge even while the Transporte is not crewed and therefore is not currently a creature.
   - This applies to Enciclopedia, deck/deckbuilder surfaces, previews, packs/rewards, hand/graveyard/support/battlefield and other surfaces using the shared renderer.
   - When crewed/animated as a creature, effective P/T continues through the normal creature-stat pipeline.
   - The fix is PRESENTATION-ONLY: `permanentTypes` / vehicle rules are untouched; printed stats do not make an uncrewed Transporte a creature.
