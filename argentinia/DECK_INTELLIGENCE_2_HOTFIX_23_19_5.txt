WHY THIS HOTFIX EXISTS
A real Tano smoke after RC2 proved the creature-floor fix worked, but exposed the opposite quality problem: a legal 60-card deck could still be 35 creatures + 23 lands + 2 Vehicles, with zero instants/sorceries/enchantments. The old role detector also treated any structured `damage` effect as removal even when the damage could only hit the opposing player. That made face-damage creatures satisfy interaction/removal quotas and allowed structurally incoherent decks to pass.

1) TARGET-AWARE INTERACTION SEMANTICS
- Player-only damage is `reach`, never creature removal.
- Damage that can legally hit a creature/permanent is creatureInteraction + removal.
- Counter, discard, destroy/exile/bounce/fight/control and sweepers retain functional interaction roles.
- Narrow interaction (artifact/enchantment/land/graveyard answers) is tracked separately from broad interaction.
- Regression cards from the real failure (Tasador de Chacarita, Sereno de Morgue, Séptimo Hijo del Barrio) no longer satisfy broadInteraction/removal.

2) HUMAN-LIKE COMPOSITION CONTRACTS
Each archetype now has a composition contract rather than only a creature minimum:
- creature minimum AND ceiling;
- Vehicle cap;
- broad interaction floor;
- creature interaction floor;
- instant/sorcery floor;
- non-creature floor;
- primary-theme density where relevant;
- typal density/support where relevant;
- dead-synergy budget by quality.

This prevents accidental 35-creature soup while still allowing legitimate creature-heavy Aggro/Typal lists when the strategy supports it. Enchantments are not mandatory by card type; functional roles are.

3) COHERENCE / TYPAL
- Typal chooses one actual subtype focus.
- Lords/payoffs are tied to literal/flexible subtype support instead of generic `typal` presence.
- Dead tribal/payoff cards are penalized, with a strict cap for Elite.
- Vehicle decks still require real crew bodies.
- Tempo viability is normalized so its broad Aggro+Spells tag union does not dominate every color pair.

4) QUALITY TIERS ARE PRODUCT BEHAVIOR
- starter: guided/basic onboarding deck. Playable and coherent, lower selection pressure, max 1 Mythic and max 6 Rare+Mythic. Designed to leave meaningful upgrade headroom.
- good: functional casual builder.
- strong: Tano Medio. Advanced/competent builder with small tolerated imperfections and lower search/selection pressure than Elite.
- elite: Tano Difícil. Full composition/coherence contracts, near-top candidate selection, largest search budget and strict dead-synergy cap.
- competitive: full-power automatic player deck profile.

Tano mapping remains:
- Fácil -> good
- Medio -> strong
- Difícil -> elite

Server-authoritative first-deck bootstrap explicitly uses `quality: starter`.
