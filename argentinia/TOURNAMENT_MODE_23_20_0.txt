ARGENTINIA v23.20.0 — TORNEO

PRODUCT CONTRACT
- Menu JUGAR exposes PARTIDA SIMPLE and TORNEO.
- Direct-elimination bracket with 16 entrants: player + 15 fictitious NPCs sampled from a trusted roster of 40.
- Rounds: Octavos -> Cuartos -> Semifinal -> Final.
- Tournament can be left/resumed only between matches. An interrupted/abandoned active match eliminates the player.
- NPC-vs-NPC results are server-simulated from rating plus Admin-configurable randomness.
- Player rival identity includes canonical name, avatar, colors, archetype and rating.

DEFAULT ROUND POLICY (all real values editable in Admin and snapshotted when a run starts)
- Octavos: 100 points, 0 packs, Medium IA, Good deck.
- Cuartos: 150 points, 0 packs, Medium IA, Strong deck.
- Semifinal: 250 points, 1 pack, Hard IA, Strong deck.
- Final: 500 points, 2 packs, Hard IA, Elite deck.
- Champion default total: 1000 points + 3 packs.
- Rewarded tournament starts/day: 1 by default; 0 means unlimited. Further starts become practice when cap is reached.
- NPC simulation randomness: 18% by default.
