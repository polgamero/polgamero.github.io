ARGENTINIA 23.21.0 — MERCADO DE PASES — PRE-RC

Adds server-authoritative player-to-player card trading with strict 1↔1 settlement.
Listings reserve one real tradable copy and may declare up to three strict BUSCO criteria:
exact card OR rarity/color filters, with optional ACEPTO CUALQUIER CARTA.

Saved decks are protected automatically: the market preserves the maximum number of copies
required by any individual saved deck, plus enhancement protection. Players do not need to
manually remove cards from decks before listing excess copies.

Mercado limits are Admin-configurable with server-enforced hard ceilings. Defaults remain:
3 BUSCO criteria, 10 offers/listing, 5 outgoing active offers and 3 completed trades/player/week.
Cancel/reject does not consume the weekly completion cap.

All Mercado user-facing copy is in Game Text. Admin Statistics tracks tradesCompleted per player
and a global completed-trades KPI. Every successful swap writes exactly one immutable economyEvent
inside the settlement transaction, so ECONOMÍA / AUDITORÍA shows a single 1↔1 operation with
usernames, UID tooltips and full trade/listing/offer/card metadata.

Backend: 37 callable Functions, schema 9, econ-23.19.5.6 unchanged, Rules 23.13.81 candidate.
Rules add server-only trade collections and reservation guards for account delete. Username
rename also fails closed while a reservation is active.

NOT RELEASED. Requires Gate03 + Gate05 + Gate07, Rules deploy, Functions 37/37, GitHub Actions
and production smoke.
