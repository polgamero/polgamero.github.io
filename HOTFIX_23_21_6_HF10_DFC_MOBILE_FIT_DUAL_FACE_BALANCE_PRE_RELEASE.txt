ARGENTINIA 23.21.6 HF10 — DFC MOBILE VIEWPORT FIT + DUAL-FACE VALUE BALANCE — PRE-RELEASE

Baseline: 23.21.6 HF9 RELEASED.
No new cards. Physical pool 900. Functions count 41. Firestore Rules 23.13.86 unchanged.

1) DFC alternate-face modal on mobile/iOS/WebKit
- Root cause: HF9 modal width was constrained by vw only; short landscape viewports could produce a 5:7 card taller than the usable viewport.
- HF10 sizes by visualViewport width AND height, derives the 5:7 width from available height, refits on viewport/window resize, respects safe-area insets, and hides the redundant CARA A/B label on short viewports.
- Goal: the complete reverse face remains inside the usable viewport without the lower card escaping off-screen.

2) Noncreature TDFC balance philosophy
- A transforming permanent should not be a blank tax until the player pays again. Face A must already provide a legitimate line of play; Face B should offer a meaningfully different or stronger plan.
- Creature TDFC fronts already provide bodies/combat and were not globally buffed again.
- Galpón Abandonado already had a useful A face ({T}:{C}) and remains unchanged from HF9 (B produces RR).

3) Balance changes
- Leyenda Susurrada en la Salamanca (4 mana Rare):
  A: whenever your creature dies, Chusmeá 1; transform now {B}{G}.
  B: whenever ANY creature dies, drain 1; transform back {B}{G}.
- Tormenta sobre la Pampa (4 mana Uncommon):
  A: every noncreature spell you cast Anticipá 1; still auto-transforms at your end step.
  B: HF9 draw-on-transform + 1 damage per noncreature spell + auto-cycle remain.
- Televisor de Tubo Encendido (3 mana Rare):
  A: {1},{T}: Anticipá 1 OR {U}{R},{T}: transform.
  B: each noncreature spell deals 2 to the rival; one trigger per cast avoids trigger-order spam; {2},{T}: transform back.
- Mate de Madrugada (2 mana Uncommon):
  A: {T}: add {C}, or {1},{T}: transform.
  B: upkeep gain 1 life, or {1},{T}: transform back. B intentionally does not inherit mana production, making A vs B a real choice.
